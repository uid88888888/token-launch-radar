Token Launch Radar

Which protocol's token would be worth the most if it launched tomorrow — and what to do to farm it first. Every protocol below records no token in the snapshot: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is the record, never proof it has none.

How this radar scores

implied value = 1,000,000 × (TVL ÷ 1,000,000)0.8 × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Size, with diminishing returns. A token’s value does not scale linearly with TVL: the biggest pools compress toward the pack, so TVL enters to the power 0.8.
  • Growth. 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +150%, weighted 0.6. A shrinking pool is marked down, a surging one marked up, never without bound.
  • Maturity. 0.55 + 0.45 × (1 − e−age/540). A protocol that held big TVL for years with no token carries a larger pent-up launch value; the factor saturates.
  • Breadth. 1 + 0.03 per chain, up to 4 chains. A launch that can reach holders on more chains is worth more.

No name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are excluded outright — WBTC is Bitcoin on Ethereum, not a protocol still waiting on a launch of its own. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart in Already trading, shown at the market’s own market cap, never at a modelled value.
